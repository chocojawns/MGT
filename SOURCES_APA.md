## Using these sources responsibly

- Ford’s 1922 book is a retrospective primary account and his own justification, not an independent audit of company practices.
- The 1914 plan was $5 **per day**, initially including conditional profit sharing; it was not a universal $5 hourly wage.
- The later Bennett/enforcer story does not establish that gang recruitment caused the earlier turnover and wage announcement.
- Model T prices span years and configurations; assembly duration is not the interval between completed vehicles.
- No verified Detroit house price, house size, grocery basket, rent, transit fare, or modern-dollar conversion is supplied here. Add the exact sources for any such figures Andy introduces verbally.
- Normal webpage references do not need fabricated access dates. If quoting a source, add a verified page, paragraph, chapter, or timestamp locator rather than inventing one.
